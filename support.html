SheetGuard AI Help

Audit and review

Open the workbook in Excel and launch SheetGuard AI. Use Audit to scan visible worksheets, then review the evidence under Recommendations. For actionable cards, preview the change and choose Accept or Decline.

Ask about a range

Select the relevant cells and enable AI data sharing in Settings. Questions send a bounded selection sample, audit summaries and recent chat to the configured reasoning service. Local audits stay in Excel.

Get help

Contact the administrator who deployed the add-in. Include your Excel platform, version, and the exact status message. Remove confidential workbook information from your report.
